Questions

Good to know.

Who can apply?

Anyone who wants real product experience: school and college students of any stream, freshers and job seekers. Whatever you are studying, there is a track for you.

Do I need to know coding?

No. You can test, do product and UX research, business analysis or data analytics. Pick what suits you, or try a bit of everything.

How much time does it take?

Not much. There are no fixed hours. Use whatever time you are free during the 10 days; even a little is enough.

What will I actually work on?

Four live products: HV AI, HV Reset, HV Hunt and HV Assess, plus new features before they launch.

What do I get at the end?

Your own dashboard on real usage data, bug reports from a live product, hands-on AI training experience and insights you can show in your portfolio, resume and interviews. Every real contribution earns points, and top contributors get gifts and token rewards.

Is a referral guaranteed?

No. For top contributors, Harsh will personally do his best to help with internship and job referrals through his network. It is a real effort, not a promise.

How do I apply?

Fill the one-minute form at thehvverse.com/beta. The HV Verse team will contact you with the next steps.
